## [0.2.0-alpha.1] - 2026-08-14

Initial public Research Alpha of Pulse.

### Added

- Installable single-host `PulseWorld` runtime with persistent per-Engram Pi
  Harness sessions, durable scheduling, causal recovery and succession.
- Task/Life centers, TaskOffer and TaskRelationship boundaries, LivingConcern,
  LivingOrientation, Living Portfolio and settlement-fenced purpose revision.
- Browser Workbench, local capability Profiles and a bounded observation API.
- Factory/field weight separation with export, checkpoint, reset and rollback.
- Exact Python/uv/Node/npm pins, no-provider-key Windows and Ubuntu CI, and
  wheel/sdist cold-install verification.
- Manifest-driven clean-root public exporter with machine-readable provenance.

### Release boundary

- The public repository begins with one root commit; it does not inherit the
  private development graph or any earlier experiment commit.
- Research logs, experiment histories, Goal/status/completion records, dated
  evidence, provider sessions and generated artifacts are not distributed.
- E0/E1 engineering coverage and the E3 release procedure are in scope. No E2
  evidence archive or E4 longitudinal claim is published.
